# British-Airways-Customer-Experience-Analysis
Business-focused analysis of 1,000+ British Airways customer reviews using Tableau and SQL to identify satisfaction drivers, service gaps, and improvement opportunities. Uncovered key insights across cabin service, entertainment, value for money, aircraft performance, and customer sentiment trends.


## Overview

This project analyzes customer reviews of British Airways to identify the key drivers of passenger satisfaction and uncover opportunities to improve customer experience.

The analysis focuses on customer ratings across multiple service dimensions, including cabin staff service, seat comfort, food quality, entertainment, ground service, and value for money. The goal is to transform customer feedback into actionable business recommendations that could support customer retention and service improvement initiatives.

---

## Business Problem

As one of the world's leading airlines, British Airways must continuously monitor customer satisfaction to remain competitive.

This analysis answers the following business questions:

- What factors drive overall customer satisfaction?
- Which service areas perform best and worst?
- How does satisfaction vary by aircraft type?
- How does customer sentiment vary across countries?
- What recommendations could improve the passenger experience?

---

## Executive Summary

### Key Findings

✅ Cabin Staff Service is the strongest performing category with an average rating of **4.2/5**

✅ Entertainment is the lowest-performing category with an average rating of **1.4/5**

✅ Value for Money scored only **2.8/5**, indicating customers often feel services do not justify ticket prices

✅ Certain aircraft types consistently receive higher passenger ratings than others

✅ Customer satisfaction varies significantly across geographic regions

✅ Review trends show fluctuating customer sentiment over time

## Analysis Areas

### 1. Customer Satisfaction Analysis

Measured customer satisfaction across:

- Overall Rating
- Cabin Staff Service
- Food Quality
- Seat Comfort
- Entertainment
- Ground Service
- Value For Money

### Key Insight

Passengers rate cabin crew positively while entertainment and value for money consistently underperform.

---

### 2. Monthly Trend Analysis

Analyzed how customer ratings changed over time.

### Key Insight

Customer sentiment fluctuates significantly throughout the review period, highlighting opportunities to investigate operational and service performance changes.

---

### 3. Geographic Analysis

Evaluated customer satisfaction across countries using an interactive map.

### Key Insight

Customer experiences vary globally, suggesting regional differences in service delivery and customer expectations.

---

### 4. Aircraft Performance Analysis

Compared ratings across aircraft models.

### Top Performing Aircraft

- Boeing 747-400
- Boeing 747
- Boeing 787
- Boeing 777

### Key Insight

Aircraft type has a measurable impact on customer experience ratings.

## Business Recommendations

### Improve Inflight Entertainment

**Finding:**
Entertainment received the lowest average rating (1.4/5).

**Recommendation:**
Invest in modern entertainment systems and enhance onboard digital content offerings.

---

### Increase Perceived Value

**Finding:**
Value For Money rated only 2.8/5.

**Recommendation:**
Review pricing strategies and improve bundled service offerings to better align customer expectations.

---

### Modernize Lower-Rated Aircraft

**Finding:**
Customer satisfaction varies by aircraft type.

**Recommendation:**
Prioritize refurbishment and modernization efforts for lower-rated aircraft.

---

### Leverage Cabin Crew Excellence

**Finding:**
Cabin staff received the highest ratings.

**Recommendation:**
Continue investing in staff training and use service excellence as a competitive advantage.
